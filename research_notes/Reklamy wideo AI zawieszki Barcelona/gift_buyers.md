## 1. Who buys gifts for male football fans in Poland: demographics, platforms, price sensitivity, budget per gift

### Takeaway
Polish surveys from 2025 show that about 80% of Poles buy Christmas gifts. A buyer gives to about 5 people and spends about 660–680 zł on all gifts together. The usual budget for one gift is 51–100 zł (2024 data), and about a quarter of buyers spend 101–200 zł (older data). So at 149 zł, a single pendant sits above the typical gift budget, and the 2 for 259 zł offer (129.50 zł each) fits the "several recipients" pattern. No Polish survey found breaks down who buys gifts *for male football fans*. The demographic profile below (women: partners and mothers) is inferred from the evidence on gender differences and on platform use.

### Inferences
- **Price positioning:** 149 zł is above the modal single-gift budget of 51–100 zł (2024) and in the upper part of the 101–200 zł bracket. The ad has to justify the price. Useful levers are the official licence, the gift box and pouch, free delivery, and the fact that it is ready to give. The 2 for 259 zł offer (129.50 zł each) works well for buyers who shop for about 5 people. Examples: "dla taty i syna", "dla brata i chłopaka", or two different clubs (Barcelona plus Real) for two fans.
- **Likely gift-buyer personas, ranked by inference.** No Polish data breaks this down for football fans.
  1. Girlfriends and wives, about 18–40, on TikTok, Instagram and Facebook. They start early (2023 CBRE data), and the risk they fear is "he won't like it".
  2. Mothers, and sometimes grandmothers, of teen or young-adult sons, about 40–60, mostly Facebook and Instagram. Facebook is the largest platform in Poland.
  3. Fathers or brothers buying a matching pair, men who lack ideas (41% of men report this barrier). These buyers respond to "gotowy pomysł" framing.
  4. Friends: a smaller group, with lower budgets.
- **How gift ads should differ from fan ads:** Fan-facing ads sell identity and pride ("noś herb"). Buyer-facing ads must solve the buyer's problem: (a) no idea what to buy, (b) fear it won't be liked or worn, (c) fear it won't arrive on time, (d) budget. The buyer may not know football, so the ad should show a man wearing it and reacting (social proof that "he'll wear it"). It should show the gift box, state the delivery deadline clearly, and present the club as a simple choice ("wybierz jego klub: Barcelona / Real / Manchester United") rather than fan insider language.

## 2. Which gift-ad formats perform best (reaction videos, gift lists, "don't know what to get him?", unboxing, carousels, couple POV)

### Takeaway
Platform guidance from Meta (2025 and 2026) and TikTok points to short, vertical, sound-on video with real people or creators, and to supplying several creative variants so the algorithm can match them to people. Unboxing and gifting-scenario creatives have some vendor-published uplift evidence, mostly not jewelry and not Poland. No independent study isolating "gift reaction" videos for jewelry or merch was found, so the format choice should be tested.

### Cited Findings
- Meta 2025 Festive Season Guide: Reels should be short, vertical and sound-on, featuring real people or creators. It also recommends working with creators, framed as a trust play rather than a reach play. — [House of Marketers](https://houseofmarketers.com/?p=15870); [JCK](https://www.jckonline.com/?p=223703)
- Meta 2026 guidance recommends supplying several creative formats so Advantage+ can match ads to people. Video accounts for more than 60% of time spent on Facebook and Instagram, which is why Meta puts Reels at the centre of awareness campaigns. — [JCK, Meta holiday guide](https://www.jckonline.com/editorial-article/meta-holiday-marketing-guide/); [JCK 2026 planning](https://www.jckonline.com/editorial-article/meta-holiday-2026-planning/)
- 58% of Gen Z shoppers find gifts through Reels and Stories, and 94% of shoppers look to creators for holiday guidance (Meta-sourced). — [JCK 2026 planning](https://www.jckonline.com/editorial-article/meta-holiday-2026-planning/)

- Racklify merchant guides: unboxing content works best in gift season when it emphasises presentation and packaging, and is less effective for low-cost commodities with minimal packaging. ### Inferences
- **Recommended format test matrix for 6–15 s AI videos.** These are inferred priorities, not proven rankings.
  1. **"Nie wiesz, co mu kupić?" problem→solution hook.** It targets the #1 barrier (lack of ideas), opens with a buyer's point of view, then cuts to the box opening and the pendant on his chest.
  2. **Reaction / reveal**: a boyfriend, dad or son opens the Vellano box and shows a happy reaction, which works as "he'll wear it" proof. For AI video, keep reactions natural and short. The faces must be generic, not resembling any player.
  3. **Unboxing / packaging close-up**: box → pouch → pendant catching light (the cyrkonie sparkle). Packaging-centred unboxing is the gift-season use case Racklify describes.
  4. **"Prezent dla dwóch kibiców" bundle story**: father and son, or two brothers with different clubs. It visualises 2 for 259 zł.
  5. **Gift-guide style** ("3 prezenty dla kibica" or "Wybierz jego klub"): a carousel or quick-cut video with three crests, which fits the "multiple creative formats" advice for Advantage+.
  6. **Couple POV / girlfriend POV** ("POV: kupiłaś mu herb jego klubu"), native to TikTok.
- Fan-facing creatives (pride, matchday, close-ups of the crest) should run in parallel. They also reach fans who may buy for themselves or ask for the pendant as a gift. Measure the two creative families separately.
- Creator-style, person-led content is consistently favoured over product-only shots in the guidance found. AI-generated "people" should be labelled according to platform AI-content rules; another researcher covers the regulations.

### Gaps
- No independent, Poland-specific or jewelry-specific performance benchmark was found for gift reaction videos against unboxing against gift-guide carousels. All uplift numbers found are vendor-published case studies in other categories.
- TikTok's EU holiday playbook figures could not be retrieved. The [NA SMB Holiday Playbook 2025](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/library/NA_SMB_Holiday_Playbook_2025.pdf) is US-focused and was not readable.

## 3. Messaging that reduces gift-buyer risk (delivery cutoffs, returns, gift box, "he'll wear it", price anchors, "for two fans"), without fake urgency

### Takeaway
The legitimate risk reducers for Polish gift buyers are a concrete delivery date tied to a real cutoff (Vellano's 18 Dec is safely ahead of the national carrier cutoff of 22 Dec), returns (14 days from receipt is the legal minimum for online purchases, and some big retailers extend Christmas returns into January), packaging that is ready to give, and clear, honest pricing. UOKiK has explicitly flagged countdown timers and "almost sold out" messages as potentially misleading pressure tactics, which matches the brand's own ban.

- UOKiK warns that countdown timers and "almost sold out" messages are often untrue and aim to pressure consumers into quick purchases. — [podatki.biz, UOKiK advice](https://www.podatki.biz/artykuly/nie-daj-sie-zrobic-na-mega-szaro-w-black-friday-porady-uokik_16_60554.htm); [infor.pl](https://www.infor.pl/prawo/prawa-konsumenta/nowosci/6373663,nieprawdziwe-promocje-np-podczas-black-friday-uokik-przypomina-o-obo.html)
- Online purchases in Poland carry a statutory right of withdrawal of 14 days from actual receipt. For in-store purchases, returns depend on store policy (UOKiK). — [infor.pl, UOKiK](https://www.infor.pl/prawo/prawa-konsumenta/nowosci/5635956,nietrafiony-prezent-zwrot-reklamacja-uokik.html); [antyweb](https://antyweb.pl/nietrafiony-prezent-uokik-podpowiada-co-z-tym-zrobic)

### Inferences
- **Compliant risk-reducer copy bank.** These are suggestions within the brand's constraints. Avoid srebro/złoto/próba, hipoalergiczna, wodoodporna, certyfikat, hologram, diamenty, ręcznie, player names, club slogans and fake scarcity.
  - Deadline, factual: "Zamów do 18.12 — dostarczymy przed Świętami." / "Gwarancja dostawy przed Wigilią przy zamówieniu do 18 grudnia." This is a real date. It leaves 4 days of buffer before the national carrier cutoff (InPost's 22 Dec in 2025) and avoids any countdown.
  - Ready to give: "Przychodzi w pudełku prezentowym Vellano z woreczkiem — gotowe pod choinkę."
  - Delivery cost: "Darmowa dostawa."
  - Choice guidance for non-fans: "Wybierz jego klub: FC Barcelona, Real Madryt lub Manchester United."
  - Licence as trust: "Oficjalnie licencjonowana zawieszka FC Barcelona." The word "licencja" is not on the banned list. Avoid "certyfikat" and "hologram".
  - Price anchor without a fake discount: "149 zł" with "2 za 259 zł — dla taty i syna / dla dwóch kibiców". Present it as a standing offer ("Zestaw dwóch zawieszek: 259 zł"), not as a "-13%" markdown, unless the Omnibus question is checked (see section 4).
  - "He'll wear it": show the pendant worn on a 51 cm chain in daily or matchday contexts. Keep any wording factual ("łańcuszek 51 cm").
  - Returns: state Vellano's actual policy (at least the statutory 14 days from receipt). If Vellano offers extended returns until a January date, say so. Amazon and Apple set the precedent, and it directly addresses the "won't be liked" fear.
- Honest urgency is acceptable and effective: dates (18.12), "ostatni tydzień zamówień z dostawą przed Świętami" (if true), and Mikołajki framing. Avoid stock-level claims unless they are literally true.

- No A/B evidence was found on which risk-reducer message (delivery date vs gift box vs returns) lifts conversion most for gift jewelry.

## 4. Polish Christmas 2026 calendar, search peaks, Q4 CPM spikes, and Omnibus discount rules

### Takeaway
The key 2026 dates are Black Friday on Fri 27 Nov, Cyber Monday on Mon 30 Nov, Mikołajki on Sun 6 Dec, Vellano's cutoff on Fri 18 Dec, and Wigilia on Thu 24 Dec. Wigilia has been a public holiday since 2025, with no courier deliveries. Polish shoppers plan early: 35% start gifts in November and 37% in the first half of December (2025 data), and only about 3% shop on 23 Dec. Meta CPMs dip in early October and peak on Cyber Monday (estimates are unverified). Any "% off" or "was/now" claim in Black Friday ads must follow the Omnibus 30-day-lowest-price rule.

### Cited Findings
**Dates (2026 weekdays computed from the calendar)**
- Black Friday 2026 = Friday 27 Nov (the day after US Thanksgiving, Thursday 26 Nov). Cyber Monday = 30 Nov. Mikołajki = Sunday 6 Dec. Vellano's cutoff = Friday 18 Dec. Wigilia = Thursday 24 Dec. 26 Dec = Saturday. These weekdays were computed locally. In 2025, Black Friday fell on 28 Nov. — [Shoper](https://www.shoper.pl/learn/?p=41385)

It covers Black Friday, Black Week, Black Month and Cyber Monday. — [podatki.biz, UOKiK](https://www.podatki.biz/artykuly/uokik-w-black-friday-jasne-ceny_52_54395.htm); [wiadomoscikosmetyczne](https://www.wiadomoscikosmetyczne.pl/biznes-kosmetyki/prawo-kosmetyczne/black-friday-w-tym-roku-pokaze-faktyczne-obnizki-2496987); [fashionbiznes](https://fashionbiznes.pl/czy-to-koniec-oszustw-nowe-przepisy-wplyna-na-tegoroczny-black-friday/)

The lowest price must be legible and not hidden in small print. — [podatki.biz](https://www.podatki.biz/artykuly/uokik-w-black-friday-jasne-ceny_52_54395.htm); [poradnikprzedsiebiorcy](https://poradnikprzedsiebiorcy.pl/-informacja-o-obnizce-cen-jak-uniknac-kary-od-uokik)

### Inferences
- **Suggested flight plan for 2026.** This is inferred from the data above.
  - **20 Oct – 15 Nov (prospecting, cheaper CPMs):** run gift-idea and fan creatives broadly to build engaged audiences and site visitors. 35% start gifts in November, and women and people aged 25–34 start earliest.
  - **16 – 30 Nov (Black Week through Cyber Monday):** CPMs peak. Keep spend efficient and lean on retargeting. If Vellano has no real price reduction, do not frame ads as "Black Friday -X%". Use "2 za 259 zł" and "darmowa dostawa" as standing value. That avoids Omnibus exposure and the fake-urgency look.
  - **1 – 6 Dec (Mikołajki, Sunday 6 Dec):** a natural hook ("na Mikołajki lub pod choinkę"), but 6 Dec delivery would need orders by about 3–4 Dec, so make no Mikołajki delivery promise unless it is operationally true.
  - **7 – 18 Dec (main gift window, 37% of buyers shop in the first half of December):** lead with "Zamów do 18.12 — dostawa przed Świętami." This is the strongest truthful urgency available.
  - **19 – 24 Dec:** switch messaging off delivery guarantees. Options: pause, or reframe toward "prezent po Świętach / na Nowy Rok" and self-purchase by fans (the "Q5" lower-CPM period).
- **Omnibus and the 2-for-259 offer:** stating "2 zawieszki za 259 zł" is a bundle price, not a markdown of the 149 zł item. Avoid "oszczędzasz 39 zł" or "-13%" unless legal confirms that multi-buy bundles fall outside the 30-day rule (see Gaps).

## 5. Targeting options on Meta and TikTok in 2026 for gift buyers; is broad plus gift-specific creative the recommended approach?

### Takeaway
Yes. On both platforms the 2026 default is algorithmic, broad targeting: Meta Advantage+ Audience, where interests are only "suggestions", and TikTok Smart+. In this setup the creative does the targeting. Detailed-targeting exclusions are gone on Meta, and many narrow interests have been merged or retired, though sources conflict on the dates. No evidence was found that Meta's old "friends of people with upcoming birthdays" or life-event gift-buyer options remain usable for this purpose. Practical setup: broad targeting in Poland with gift-buyer creatives and fan creatives in separate ads, plus retargeting of site visitors and add-to-carts.

### Cited Findings
**Meta**
- For sales, leads and app objectives, Advantage+ Audience is reportedly the default and cannot be switched off. Detailed targeting acts only as a suggestion that Meta can expand beyond. Location and minimum age remain hard limits. — [Conversios 2026 guide](https://www.conversios.io/blog/meta-advantage-audience-vs-detailed-targeting-2026-guide/); [Linear Design](https://lineardesign.com/blog/metas-advantage-audience/); [AdBid](https://adbid.me/blog/meta-advantage-plus-audience-guide-2026) (third-party blogs; which blog makes which claim is approximate)

### Inferences
- **Recommended structure (inferred):**
  - Meta: one Advantage+ sales campaign in Poland, with separate ads or creative groups for (a) gift buyers (girlfriend/partner, mother, father-and-son bundle, "nie wiesz, co mu kupić?") and (b) fans (pride, crest close-ups).
  - Keep age as a hard minimum only. Let the creative self-select women 25–55 and so on, rather than gender-locking, because men also buy for sons and brothers.
  - Use interests such as "FC Barcelona", "football", "jewelry" or "gifts" only as suggestions, if they are still available.
  - Run a retargeting layer, or let Advantage+ handle existing customers, with deadline creatives in 7–18 Dec.
- TikTok: Smart+ with targeting automation on, plus a manual test ad group (women 18–44 or "gifting" and "football" interests, at most 2–3) to see whether manual targeting beats broad in this niche.
- Creative-as-targeting signals: Polish voice-over or captions in the buyer's voice ("Kupiłam mu…", "Dla mojego taty i brata"), visual gift cues (box, wrapping, tree), and explicit "prezent dla kibica" text. These help the algorithm find buyers who are not themselves fans.
