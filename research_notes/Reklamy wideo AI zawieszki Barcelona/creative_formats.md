## 1. Which formats dominate top-performing jewelry / men's chain ads in 2025–2026, and which are reported to convert (not just get views)?

### Takeaway
No independent dataset ranks jewelry ad formats by conversion. The best available 2025–2026 evidence, all from vendors or agencies, points to a funnel split. Creator-style or UGC video, including unboxing, on-body and close-up demo clips, wins attention and cold-traffic clicks. Catalog/DPA and static product ads ("enriched" with lifestyle frames, reviews or occasion themes) take most of the actual conversions and the cheapest clicks. Hooks that challenge something (a problem, a price, a known weakness) beat generic gifting lines. The category's dominant claim is durability (no tarnish, fading or skin discoloration), and Vellano is barred from most of the words that claim relies on.

### Cited Findings
**Jewelry-specific reports and teardowns (2026)**
- QuickAds analysed 1,500+ active Meta ads from 30 D2C and legacy jewellery brands (US, UK, India), Q4 2025–Q1 2026. Each ad was classified by format, hook type, funnel position and creative approach. The company calls its metrics "observed pattern ranges, not projections." — [QuickAds D2C Jewelry Ads Report 2026](https://www.quickads.ai/jewelry-d2c-ads-reports-2026); [QuickAds PDF](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/64b11f315314a4ef84c12744/6ab534603634e0da2c76ecc1_quickads-jewelry-report-2026.pdf)
  - UGC drives 1.5–2.5% CTR on cold traffic. Enriched DPA/catalog drives 60–70% of actual conversions at the bottom of the funnel, at 3–6x ROAS. The report's summary line is "UGC wins attention. Enriched catalog wins revenue." — [QuickAds](https://www.quickads.ai/jewelry-d2c-ads-reports-2026)
  - Enriched catalog ads (lifestyle imagery, branded frames, review overlays, urgency badges) beat plain white-background catalog ads by 34–54% ROAS on the same audience. — [QuickAds](https://www.quickads.ai/jewelry-d2c-ads-reports-2026)
  - Studio shots work at mid-funnel consideration (2–3x ROAS) and lifestyle imagery works for retargeting, but the report says "neither should be your cold traffic format." — [QuickAds](https://www.quickads.ai/jewelry-d2c-ads-reports-2026)
  - The report cites Mejuri: "UGC→DPA sequencing lifted iROAS by 57%", and argues that sequenced ROAS matters more than CTR. — [QuickAds](https://www.quickads.ai/jewelry-d2c-ads-reports-2026)
  - Search summary of the report: "UGC Video at 2.2 CTR versus 0.6 for static images" on Meta. — [QuickAds PDF via search summary](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/64b11f315314a4ef84c12744/6ab534603634e0da2c76ecc1_quickads-jewelry-report-2026.pdf). **Conflict:** H&CO's single-account data below found static ads gave the cheapest clicks and the highest CTR.

- Brenton Way, "Jewelry Ads Creative Report, September 2026": 150+ ad examples reviewed, with eight teardowns of Meta creative from July–August 2026. Seven of the eight are video. The strongest openers "challenge something rather than describe it": calling out a problem, anchoring against a competitor's price, admitting a weakness, or using the category's own language. The "conventional gifting line is the softest hook in the set and has the least scale behind it." Durability is the dominant category claim: tarnishing, fading and skin discoloration are pre-empted across unrelated advertisers. ## 2. Hook structures (first 1–3 s), ideal ad lengths, captions/text overlays, sound, CTAs on TikTok vs Meta in 2026, and what Meta Andromeda / Advantage+ and TikTok Smart+ / Symphony reward

### Takeaway
Put the product or key message in the first 3 seconds (TikTok's own data). Aim for roughly 15–30 s on Meta and roughly 9–34 s on TikTok, with no consensus on the exact length. Burn in captions and keep text inside the 9:16 safe zones. Both platforms' AI delivery systems now reward many conceptually different creatives (different hook, angle, persona, format or visual world), not minor text or colour variants. For a small account, that means roughly 6–15 genuinely distinct concepts, refreshed every 2–3 weeks.

### Cited Findings

- TikTok: "over 63% of the videos with the highest click-through rate highlight their key message or product within the first 3 seconds." — [TikTok Auction Ads Creative Tips PDF](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/library/Auction_Ads_Creative_Tips.pdf) **[undated official doc, likely pre-2025]**
- Third-party summaries of TikTok guidance say to introduce the product or offer in the first 3 s and prioritise the hook in the first 6 s. TikTok counts "hook rate" as 3-second views ÷ impressions. — [Stackmatix](https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/tiktok-creative-best-practices-2026); [House of Marketers](https://houseofmarketers.com/importance-of-tiktok-ad-hooks-first-3-seconds/)
- The most common hook mistake is using copy written for Meta, a website banner or a product page on TikTok. — [Zeely hooks](https://zeely.ai/blog/hooks-for-tiktok-video-ads/) / [search summary](https://houseofmarketers.com/importance-of-tiktok-ad-hooks-first-3-seconds/)
- QuickAds' hook library claims specific hooks averaged 23–41% higher CTR than generic copy (vendor). — [QuickAds](https://www.quickads.ai/jewelry-report)

**Hook-rate / thumb-stop benchmarks**

- Motion **[2025 thresholds]**: thumbstop below 25% "needs work", 25–35% solid, above 35% strong. Hold rate above 60% is strong, 40–50% average. — [Sepia Lab citing Motion](https://sepia-lab.com/en/blog/hook-rate-benchmarks). ### Inferences
- **Hook rule for Vellano's concepts:** the crest or the glint should be visible in frame 1–2 (0–1 s), and the text hook should name the audience ("Kibic Barcy?" / "Prezent dla kibica Barcy?"). The owner's "3, 2, 1" countdown works only if the necklace or crest is already partly visible during the countdown, or the countdown is compressed to about 1.5 s.
- **Targets:** cold-traffic thumbstop ≥25–30% on Meta and ≥30–33% on TikTok. Hold/completion near the 18% (Meta) / 24% (TikTok) medians. Cold CTR ≥1.5% on Meta Reels (jewelry UGC 1.5–2.5%).
- **Lengths:** produce a 6–8 s "sparkle/reveal" cut, a 12–15 s core UGC cut and a 21–30 s "story" gift cut, and let delivery decide.
- **Diversity under Andromeda/Smart+:** changing only the text over identical AI footage likely counts as the same ad. Distinct concepts should differ in visual world (apartment/hoodie vs stadium-night street vs Christmas living room vs macro studio), persona (fan vs gift-buying partner/parent) and format (UGC voiceover, POV, slideshow, macro loop).
- CTA: soft, platform-native ("Link w bio / Kup teraz – 149 zł, 2 za 259 zł") plus a real Christmas delivery cut-off date. Avoid countdown timers or "ostatnie sztuki" unless true.

### Gaps
- No primary Meta document confirming 2026 creative-volume numbers, similarity thresholds or default-on Advantage+ enhancements.
- No 2026 official TikTok length recommendation found. Sound-on vs sound-off performance data for 2026 not found.
- No jewelry-specific hook-rate or thumbstop benchmark found. All benchmarks above are cross-category.
- No Polish-market creative benchmarks (CPM/CTR) found.

## 4. Is AI-generated UGC (AI avatars/actors) performing as well as real UGC in 2026? Data on conversion, backlash, "AI slop" fatigue, and what makes AI ads look authentic vs fake

### Takeaway
The best independent 2026 evidence (Ipsos/Syracuse, May 2026) shows human-made ads beating AI versions by about 14% short-term and 17% long-term. The gap is smallest, and AI sometimes wins, in functional, product-focused ads. Viewers mostly can't tell AI from human. Vendor head-to-heads put AI UGC slightly behind human UGC on CTR (about 15–25% lower) but far cheaper, so the consensus is hybrid use. Polish 2026 data shows an "AI-generated" label cuts trust for about half of consumers and about three-quarters of the youngest. Holiday AI ads with people and emotion (McDonald's NL 2025, Coca-Cola) drew the strongest backlash. EU AI Act Art. 50 disclosure duties apply from 2 Aug 2026, and TikTok requires AIGC disclosure in ads.

### Cited Findings
**Performance**
- Ipsos and Syracuse University (May 2026) took 10 major-brand 30-second ads, had Gemini reverse-engineer each brief and made AI versions with Sora 2. All 20 ads were tested with 3,000 US consumers. Human-made ads were 14% stronger on short-term effectiveness (Creative Effect Index) and 17% stronger long-term (Equity Effect Index). AI ads did better on functional, product-benefit ads (AI Febreze and Herbal Essences ads beat the human originals). Only 25% of AI-ad viewers were at least somewhat confident the spot was AI-made (Syracuse's summary gives 13%, an unresolved discrepancy). — [Ipsos](https://www.ipsos.com/en-us/ai-ads-are-good-enough-and-thats-problem); [Ipsos PDF](https://www.ipsos.com/sites/default/files/ct/publication/documents/2026-05/Ipsos_SU_POV-AI_Ads_Just_Good_Enough.pdf); [Syracuse News](https://news.syr.edu/2026/05/18/newhouse-research-finds-ai-ads-fall-short-on-sales-impact/); [Phys.org](https://phys.org/news/2026-05-ai-ads-indistinguishable-human-dont.html)
- Billo's 10-vs-10 test (Billo sells human UGC): human UGC CTR was 1.8% vs 1.5% for AI on TikTok and 2.3% vs 2.0% on Meta. Creative spend was $2,100 for human vs $40 for AI, and AI results were more consistent across videos. — [Billo](https://billo.app/blog/ai-generated-ads-performance/)
- Practitioner test (on an AI vendor's blog): AI UGC at about 2.3–2.7% CTR vs 3.0–3.5% for human UGC, while monthly output rose sharply. — [Creatify](https://creatify.ai/blog/the-8-best-ai-ugc-ad-tools-in-2026)
- Vendor aggregates put AI UGC at about 70–90% of top real-creator CTR, or 85–110% in cold testing (unverified). One source reports heavy AI use cut ROAS within 60 days through lower LTV. — [Superscale](https://superscale.ai/learn/ai-vs-traditional-ugc-complete-comparison/); [Playcut](https://playcut.ai/blog/ai-ugc-vs-real-ugc/)
- Arcads' widely shared "$14K → $1.3M" script case has no control group. A roundup cites about 26% lower CPA for AI creative in e-commerce, mostly attributed to faster testing volume. — [Omid Saffari](https://omidsaffari.com/blog/best-ai-ugc-ad-tools-2026)
- Jewelry fit: avatar tools such as Arcads are weak on close-up product detail and on-model shots because they centre on talking heads. Hybrid recommendation: AI hooks plus real product footage, noting "actor fatigue" with pure-AI campaigns. — [Sepia Lab](https://sepia-lab.com/en/blog/best-ai-ugc-tools-for-jewelry-brands); [Wireflow](https://www.wireflow.ai/blog/arcads-vs-creatify)

**Trust / backlash**
- Animoto 2026: 36% of consumers say AI video lowers their trust in a brand. Gartner: 50% would rather buy from brands that avoid GenAI in consumer-facing content. — [search summary of Superscale/Animoto](https://superscale.ai/learn/ai-vs-traditional-ugc-complete-comparison/); [StockTwits](https://stocktwits.com/news-articles/markets/equity/cocacola-mcdonalds-learn-that-ai-slop-ads-just-wont-cut-it-emotionally-with-consumers/cLeEwZXRE8n)
- McDonald's Netherlands pulled its 45-second AI Christmas ad (TBWA\Neboko / The Sweetshop) within about a week in December 2025 after it was called "creepy", "unsettling" and "inauthentic". — [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/ai-platforms-assistants/mcdonalds-pulls-ai-generated-christmas-ad-after-backlash-over-soulless-visuals-and-holiday-chaos); [Deseret](https://www.deseret.com/business/2025/12/15/mcdonalds-faced-backlash-for-ai-advert/)
- Coca-Cola's AI "Holidays Are Coming" remakes (2024 **[pre-2025]** and 2025) were criticised as "soulless". Accounts conflict on whether the 2024 ad was pulled. One sentiment analysis reports positive reactions fell from 23.8% to 10.2%. — [NN/G](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ai-ad/); [StockTwits](https://stocktwits.com/news-articles/markets/equity/cocacola-mcdonalds-learn-that-ai-slop-ads-just-wont-cut-it-emotionally-with-consumers/cLeEwZXRE8n)
- Backlash centred on emotional hollowness rather than AI use as such, and was strongest for holiday campaigns where audiences expect warmth. — [StockTwits](https://stocktwits.com/news-articles/markets/equity/cocacola-mcdonalds-learn-that-ai-slop-ads-just-wont-cut-it-emotionally-with-consumers/cLeEwZXRE8n); [NN/G](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ai-ad/)

**Poland**
- Tears of Joy (agency study, 2026): 53% say an "wygenerowano przez AI" label lowers their trust in the brand, and 48% trust the information in such ads less. Among the youngest, 73% say the label lowers trust and 38% abandon the purchase. — [aboutmarketing.pl](https://aboutmarketing.pl/tears-of-joy-polowa-polakow-nie-ufa-reklamom-z-ai-badanie/); [Wirtualnemedia](https://www.wirtualnemedia.pl/mlodzi-nie-kupuja-reklamy-od-ai-prawie-polowa-deklaruje-rezygnacje-z-zakupu,7339970891114976a)
- ARC Rynek i Opinia (2025): 35% are neutral toward marketers using AI, 21% enthusiastic, 26% critical. 71% expect transparency about AI use in ads. — [Press.pl](https://www.press.pl/tresc/86682,polacy-chca-byc-informowani_-czy-reklame-wygenerowala-ai)

**Disclosure rules**
- EU AI Act Art. 50 applies from 2 Aug 2026. A grace period until 2 Dec 2026 covers only the machine-readable marking duty, for AI systems placed on the market before 2 Aug 2026. Deployers must disclose deep fakes. Content made before 2 Aug 2026 need not be relabelled. — [European Commission FAQ](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/faqs/transparency-obligations-under-article-50-ai-act); [Addleshaw Goddard](https://www.addleshawgoddard.com/en/insights/insights-briefings/2026/technology/ai-transparency-ai-act-what-businesses-need-know-before-2-august-2026/)
- An invisible watermark from the AI tool is reportedly not enough on its own for ads; a visible disclosure label is needed (secondary). — [Gromerce](https://gromerce.com/en/blog/eu-ai-act-article-50-enforcement-ai-ads-2026)
- TikTok ads (secondary sources, conflicting dates: a July 21, 2026 policy update vs an April 2026 policy version): mandatory disclosure covers synthetic faces, cloned or AI voices, AI backgrounds and photorealistic synthetic product shots. Methods are the AIGC toggle, on-screen text, caption or sticker. Minor edits (lighting, colour, background removal) generally need no label. Undisclosed AIGC means rejection or restriction. — [Common Thread Co](https://commonthreadco.com/blogs/coachs-corner/tiktok-ai-ad-disclosure-rules-ecommerce-2026); [AuditSocials](https://www.auditsocials.com/blog/tiktok-ai-content-disclosure-rules-2026)
- Meta: mandatory AI disclosure is reportedly specific to political and social-issue ads, with an Ads Manager disclosure control. Meta's "AI info" label rules for ads were not confirmed. — [82dash](https://blog.82dash.com/ai-ad-label-rules-meta-google-tiktok/); [Novoads](https://novoads.ai/en/blog/ai-ad-label-rules-2026)

**What makes AI ads look fake / authentic**
- Visual tells: over-smoothed waxy skin, dead or unblinking eyes, bad lip sync, stiff motion, face drift, and hands (many joints, self-occlusion, must be right in every frame). Logos, labels and on-screen text bend, move or flicker between frames. For jewelry, small reflective details and true sparkle are hard to render. — [Digital Synopsis](https://digitalsynopsis.com/tools/ai-videos-look-fake-how-to-fix/); [Oakgen](https://oakgen.ai/blog/realistic-ai-ugc-ads-checklist); [UGCGen](https://ugcgen.ai/ugc-for-jewelry-brands)
- Script tells: perfect grammar, no contractions, zero filler words. Backgrounds that are too perfect. Product inaccuracy (changed label or packaging) destroys trust. — [AdMakeAI](https://admakeai.com/blog/what-is-ai-ugc-ad); [Linocut](https://linocut.ai/blogs/how-to-make-ai-ugc-ads-not-ai-slop/)
- Fixes: composite captions, logos and CTAs in post instead of letting the model paint them. Use matte skin, neutral light, phone-camera framing and "one imperfect beat". Frame hands out or give them a simple object. Reject takes with warped packaging, hands or jewelry. (8frame's article carries a correction that some of its rules were unmeasured.) — [Oakgen](https://oakgen.ai/blog/realistic-ai-ugc-ads-checklist); [Salesaicourse](https://salesaicourse.com/why-ai-video-looks-fake/); [8frame](https://www.8frame.co/blog/why-ai-video-looks-fake)

### Inferences
- **Best AI use for Vellano: product-centric shots** (sparkle macro, pendant rotation, chain on a neck with the face cropped, gift box opening), animated from real product photos with the real crest composited in post. This matches the Ipsos finding that AI does best on functional, product-focused ads, and avoids the logo-warping weakness. A distorted licensed crest is both a trust problem and probably a licence-compliance problem.
- **Highest-risk AI use: a photoreal AI "customer" giving a first-person testimonial** ("kupiłem to i…") or an AI gift-reaction scene at Christmas. It combines the deep-fake disclosure duty, the Polish trust penalty for labelled AI (53% / 73% among the young), the holiday-warmth backlash pattern, and likely EU consumer-law issues with fabricated testimonials (not researched here; needs legal check). The owner's "hoodie guy counting 3-2-1" with a photoreal AI person falls into the disclosure zone. Keeping the face cropped or out of focus, or using a stylised look, reduces both risk and uncanniness.
- **AI voiceover:** write Polish scripts with natural fillers and colloquialisms, not ad-perfect grammar. Use a narrator or "friend tip" voice rather than a fake purchaser. Add on-screen captions as text in post.
- Plan the label in from the start: TikTok AIGC toggle; on Meta, a caption line such as "wideo stworzone z pomocą AI". Assume the label will reduce trust somewhat, which is another reason to push the product-only AI formats.

### Gaps
- No credible jewelry-specific AI-vs-human UGC conversion test found. All head-to-heads are vendor-run.
- Exact effective date and wording of TikTok's 2026 AIGC ad policy not confirmed from TikTok's own help centre. Meta's EU handling of AI labels on ads not confirmed.
- Whether an AI-generated person in a realistic apartment legally counts as a "deep fake" under Art. 50(4) for a product ad was not resolved. It needs legal review.
- The 79% "companies should disclose AI" figure appears only in a secondary blog. The Ipsos detection figure conflicts (25% vs 13%).

## 5. Hooks and angles for passionate sports fans (identity, rivalry, match-day, "real fans only", tag-a-friend) and how brands time ads to matches

### Takeaway
Evidence for specific fan hooks is mostly practitioner commentary. Identity ("people read you as one of us") and friendly rivalry/banter are the consistently recommended levers, with antagonism discouraged for brand safety. TikTok's World Cup 2026 data shows fans consume reactions, behind-the-scenes and fan perspectives more than highlights. No published data was found on match-day ad timing or "tag a friend" conversion.

### Cited Findings
- Fan merch purchases are framed as identity purchases: fans wear club items so others read them as group members. Limited-run rivalry pieces reportedly convert "several times better" during derby weeks (unsourced multiple). Marketers should amplify pride, friendly banter and civic identity rather than antagonism. — [SportCenter "Merchant of Matchday"](https://sportcenter.us/merchant-of-matchday-the-insights-behind-fan-merchandise-mot)
- Strong sports campaigns build on what fans care about: pride, rivalry, identity, perseverance, community. — [Designity](https://www.designity.com/blog/the-best-sports-marketing-campaigns); [The Drum](https://www.thedrum.com/news/how-sports-marketing-became-all-about-fandom-and-community-not-just-sponsorship)
- Tie a premium release or giveaway to a narrative such as a "rivalry night" theme. TGR Haas F1's click-to-WhatsApp quiz (vendor case) reported 80% lower cost per conversion. No source on "tag a friend" ad conversion was found. — [search summary](https://sportcenter.us/merchant-of-matchday-the-insights-behind-fan-merchandise-mot); [Infobip](https://www.infobip.com/blog/how-to-win-at-sports-marketing)
- TikTok World Cup 2026 (self-reported): 1.2 trillion views of tournament content and 12M+ #FIFAWorldCup posts (+~1,700% vs all of 2022). TikTok's sport lead says people watch "reactions, tactical breakdowns, behind-the-scenes access, and fan perspectives", not only highlights. TikTok claims a 42% higher likelihood of tuning in to live matches after watching sports content, and that 59% find sports content on TikTok often more entertaining than the games. — [TikTok Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/fifa-world-cup-2026-content-reaches-more-than-1-trillion-views-on-tiktok?lang=en); [TikTok Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/fifa-world-cup-2026-as-seen-on-tiktok?lang=en); [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/07/21/youtube-tiktok-2026-fifa-world-cup-viewership-results/)
- Blinkfire (11 Jun–19 Jul 2026, all platforms): TikTok held 70.7% of total World Cup engagement share, and four of the five top accounts by engagement per post were TikTok channels. — [Blinkfire](https://www.blinkfire.com/d/view_upload/fifa_world_cup_2026_recap_report)
- Fan-creator posting guidance (blog, not performance data): 2–3 posts per match day (pre-match, half-time reaction, post-match). — [OneValeFan](https://www.onevalefan.co.uk/2026/08/from-matchday-moments-to-viral-videos-how-football-fans-can-grow-their-tiktok-audience/)
- TikTok's sports blog recommends native videos around big events **[pre-2025, ~2 years old]**. — [TikTok for Business: Sports](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog/sports-marketing-how-to-win-the-big-game)
- Polish platform reach (Gemius "Social Media 2026"): Facebook 85.4% of internet users, YouTube 84.3%, Instagram 54.8%, TikTok 51.5%. TikTok users average 87 min 58 s per day vs about 50 min on Facebook. Monthly ad contacts: Facebook 16.2bn, YouTube 10.7bn, TikTok 8.8bn, Instagram 3.2bn. Instagram has the highest viewability at 82.7%. — [PBI](https://pbi.org.pl/informacje-prasowe/raport-social-media-2026-juz-dostepny/); [natemat](https://natemat.pl/657655,raport-social-media-2026-polacy-scrolluja-ponad-2-h-dziennie-dzieci-jeszcze-dluzej)

Among 25+, Facebook, Instagram and YouTube dominate. 61% look for product info on Facebook and 36% on Instagram. — [IAB Polska PDF](https://www.iab.org.pl/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Raport-Polacy-w-social-mediach_IAB_Polska_2026.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Fan audience (TikTok + Reels):** identity hooks ("Dla tych, co oglądają każdy mecz Barcy" / "Herb, który nosisz bliżej serca niż szalik") and friendly rivalry. Vellano sells both Barça and Real, so "Barça czy Real? Wybierz stronę" is natural comment bait, and the 259 zł bundle supports a mixed household or two-friends scenario. Keep it banter, not hostility, and avoid chants and slogans. Fan-perspective and reaction framing (watching the match in a hoodie, then the reveal) matches what TikTok reports fans consume.
- **Gift-buyer audience (Meta FB/IG, older and 25+):** Facebook and Instagram dominate reach and product-info search among Polish 25+ users, so gift-buyer creative should be built Meta-first, in 15–30 s, with a gift-box unboxing or "what he'll open on Christmas Eve" POV. Use problem framing ("Ma już koszulkę, szalik i kubek…") rather than the soft generic gift line. Intergenerational warmth (parent to young fan, as in Man City 2025) is better shown with product and hands than with AI faces, given the AI holiday backlash.
- **Match timing (inference, unverified):** the strongest moments are Clásico weeks and big Barça matches. Practical options are posting organic or Spark content before and after matches, refreshing creative around derby weeks, and using match-day themed variants as separate concepts for Andromeda/Smart+ diversity. No source confirms a measurable lift from match-day timing for merch ads.
- "Real fans only" gatekeeping hooks may alienate gift buyers, who are by definition often not fans. Keep them in fan-audience creative only.

### Gaps
- No published data on match-day ad timing, dayparting around fixtures, or El Clásico merch campaign results on Meta or TikTok.
- No data on "tag a friend" or comment-bait conversion for fan merch.
- No Polish football-fan ad creative research found. The fixture calendar itself was not researched here; it is presumably covered by another workstream.
