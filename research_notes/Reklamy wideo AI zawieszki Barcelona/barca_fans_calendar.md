# FC Barcelona fans in Poland (2026) and the club's calendar, 19 Oct – 24 Dec 2026, for timing ads

*Research date: 19 October 2026. Method note: in this environment WebFetch could not reach outside hosts (DNS/proxy refusal for wikipedia.org, uefa.com, laliga.com). All findings below come from WebSearch result pages and snippets, cross-checked between sources where possible. Times are given as Polish time (Poland and peninsular Spain share a time zone, so Spanish local time is Polish time). Where English-language sites (LaLiga en-GB, ESPN US) show other times, they were converted or flagged.*

## 1. Polish players at FC Barcelona in 2026–27 and how this affects Polish interest

### Takeaway
Robert Lewandowski is **no longer at Barça**. He left when his contract ended in June 2026 and joined Chicago Fire (MLS), announced 29 June 2026. **Wojciech Szczęsny is still in the squad** as backup goalkeeper on a contract to 30 June 2027. **Ewa Pajor** (Barça Femení, contract to June 2027) is now the most prominent Polish starter at the club. Polish interest in the men's team has lost its main "Lewy" driver. I found no hard data measuring how big the drop is.

### Cited Findings
**Lewandowski**
- In mid-May 2026 Lewandowski announced he would leave Barcelona when his contract expired at the end of 2025–26. Barça had been willing to extend him on reduced terms. In Barcelona he won three La Liga titles (including 2025–26) and the 2025 Copa del Rey, and scored 119 goals in 191 games since joining from Bayern in 2022 — [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/soccer%20/story/_/id/48769101/robert-lewandowski-leave-barcelona-end-season); [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/sports/2026/5/16/robert-lewandowski-to-leave-barcelona-with-his-mission-complete); [TVP World](https://tvpworld.com/93286193/polish-star-robert-lewandowski-says-he-will-leave-barcelona)
- His new club is **Chicago Fire (MLS)**, announced 29 June 2026, with designated-player status. Reports differ on the contract length: to the end of 2027/28 vs. two years to December 2028 vs. two years plus a one-year option — [PolsatNews](https://www.polsatnews.pl/wiadomosc/2026-06-29/robert-lewandowski-w-nowym-klubie-jest-potwierdzenie/); [PolsatSport](https://www.polsatsport.pl/robert-lewandowski/2026-06-29/koniec-spekulacji-znamy-nowy-klub-roberta-lewandowskiego/); [WP SportoweFakty](https://sportowefakty.wp.pl/pilka-nozna/1259517/to-juz-pewne-robert-lewandowski-ma-nowy-klub)
- A Mundo Deportivo poll of Barça socios found 58% rated his time at the club "excellent" and almost 40% "very good" — [Polskie Radio 24](https://polskieradio24.pl/sport/kibice-barcelony-ocenili-lewandowskiego-ta-ankieta-nie-pozostawia-zludzen)
- An Ariadna panel poll for WP found that over half of Polish respondents (51%) had *not* watched Lewandowski in Barça matches. A later WP poll after his MLS move ran under the headline "75% say no" about watching his matches, and the summary said no MLS viewing boom is expected — [WP SportoweFakty (75%)](https://sportowefakty.wp.pl/pilka-nozna/1263757/polacy-stanowczo-o-meczach-lewandowskiego-75-proc-jest-na-nie); [WP SportoweFakty (Ariadna)](https://sportowefakty.wp.pl/pilka-nozna/1251827/dwa-pytania-o-lewandowskiego-i-arabie-nie-do-wiary-co-uwazaja-polacy); see also [o2.pl poll](https://www.o2.pl/sport/lewandowski-nie-przedluzyl-kontraktu-tylu-polakow-dalej-chce-go-w-barcelonie-7275460113168672a)

### Inferences
- With Lewandowski gone, the "Polish hero at Barça" story that drove 2022–26 interest has largely ended for the men's team. Szczęsny is a backup and rarely plays, so Polish attention on Barça is likely to come back to the **club itself** (Clásico, Champions League nights, star young players) and to **Pajor/Barça Femení**. This fits the brief well: ads that do not depend on player names lose little.
- The core Polish Barça fan existed before Lewandowski (penyas date from 2004–2007, see section 2). Casual "Lewy followers" may drift away, but loyal fans are the most likely buyers of a 149 zł crest pendant.
- The Ariadna/WP figures suggest that even at his peak, most Poles did not regularly watch Barça matches. Target the self-identified fan segment (interest targeting plus lookalikes), not general football audiences.

### Gaps
- I found no 2026 data (survey, TV ratings, social follower split) that measures the change in Polish interest in Barça after Lewandowski left.
- No Polish players in Barça's men's first team besides Szczęsny were found. I did not verify La Masia academy lists.
- I could not confirm whether Szczęsny has played any official 2026/27 match so far.

## 2. How big Barça's Polish fanbase is: surveys, social media, penyas, Google Trends

### Takeaway
There is **no recent representative survey** of foreign-club support in Poland. The last one I found (2014) put Barça second (20.9%) behind Real Madrid (24.6%). Organised fandom is small but long-established: two official penyas plus at least one newer one, and fan pages of roughly 36k–161k followers each. Google Trends Poland shows strong spikes on match days, for example 50k+ searches for "real betis – fc barcelona" on 17 Oct 2026.

### Cited Findings
- **Pentagon Research, July 2014, n=1,002 Polish internet users:** Real Madrid 24.6%, FC Barcelona 20.9%, Borussia Dortmund 16.3%, Bayern 6.3%, then English clubs (Arsenal, Man Utd, Chelsea, Liverpool). Atlético and Juventus closed the top 10 — [Wirtualnemedia](https://www.wirtualnemedia.pl/real-barcelona-i-borussia-najpopularniejszymi-zagranicznymi-klubami-pilkarskimi-w-polsce,7172244570445953a)
- **Official penyas in Poland:**
  - **Fan Club Barça Polska (FCBP)**, official penya no. **1782**, based in Żory. Sources give founding as 2004 (Olsztyn start) or 2005, with club accreditation in 2006 — [FCBP FAQ](https://fcbp.pl/club/faq/25-czlonkostwo); [DumaKatalonii.pl](https://dumakatalonii.pl/fankluby/); [FCBarca.com](https://www.fcbarca.com/24150-fan-club-barca-polska-rekrutacja-czas-na-ciebie.html)
  - **Penya Barcelonista de Varsòvia** (Warsaw, founded 2007, linked to blaugrana.pl) — [DumaKatalonii.pl](https://dumakatalonii.pl/fankluby/); [FC Barcelona penyes list](https://penyes.fcbarcelona.com/en/our-penyes/penyes-list/world)
  - Blaugrana.pl reported that a "new Polish penya" was officially accepted by the club. The date and name are not visible in the snippet — [Blaugrana.pl](https://blaugrana.pl/6250-Nowa-polska-penya-oficjalnie-zaakceptowana-przez-klub)
  - **661 Polish penya members** travelled to the Allianz Arena to support Barça (date not visible in the snippet) — [FC Barcelona Penyes](https://penyes.fcbarcelona.com/en/news/2788426/661-polish-penya-members-headed-to-allianz-arena-to-support-fc-barcelona)
- **Social media sizes (third-party snapshots, dates vary):**
  - Fan Club Barça Polska Facebook page: about **161k** followers (161,137 to 161,148 in snapshots) — [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/fcbpolska/)
  - "FC Barcelona Polska" Facebook news page: **36,060** followers in one snapshot, while an Instagram reference suggests about 133k (conflicting) — [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/BarcelonaPolska/)
  - FCBarca.com Facebook: **125,517** followers. The site calls itself the largest Polish-language Barça service — [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/FCBarcacom/); [Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/pl/podcast/podcast-fcbarca-com/id1466717424?l=pl)
  - Barca News PL Instagram: about **63k** followers — [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/barcanewspl/)
  - FCBarca.com Instagram: about 1.6k followers in a cached listing, possibly outdated. Its TikTok is @fcbarcacom (follower count not found) — [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/fcbarca_com/)
- **Google Trends Poland:** on 17 Oct 2026 "real betis – fc barcelona" was trending with **50k+ searches**. Related queries included "fc barcelona" and "barcelona mecze" (+100% to +800%) — [Google Trends PL trending](https://trends.google.com/trending?geo=PL&hl=en-US)
- **Global Google Trends "Real Madrid-Barcelona"** (not Poland-specific) peaked at 100 in late Oct 2025 and 65 in mid-Jan 2026, which shows Clásico weeks drive the largest search spikes — [Google Trends](https://trends.google.com/trends/explore?q=Real+Madrid-Barcelona)

### Inferences
- Barça and Real Madrid have long been the top two foreign clubs in Poland (2014 data). Barça probably gained ground in 2022–26 thanks to Lewandowski, but this cannot be shown with data. Treat "Barça is one of the 2–3 most-followed foreign clubs in Poland" as a reasonable working assumption, not a measured fact.
- Polish fan pages reach about 0.4M combined followers (with overlap). This is the hard-core community, much smaller than the casual audience that Meta and TikTok interest targeting can reach.
- Match days produce measurable search spikes in Poland (50k+ for a regular league away game). Clásico and Champions League nights should be higher.

### Gaps
- No 2022–2026 representative Polish poll (CBOS, IBRiS, SW Research etc.) on foreign-club preference was found.
- No Poland-only Google Trends comparison of "Barcelona" vs "Real Madryt" could be extracted, because fetch access was blocked. Run it manually: trends.google.com, geo=PL, 5-year range.
- FC Barcelona's official follower split by country (Poland) was not found.

## 3. Who Polish Barça fans are (age, gender) and where they are online; large communities for partnerships

### Takeaway
I found no demographic study specific to Barça. General Polish football fandom **skews male** (about 2–3x more men than women call themselves fans) and is concentrated in the **25–44** age band. The ad platforms skew the other way: Polish Instagram and TikTok ad audiences are about 59–62% female, with **25–34** as the largest band on both. Communities with partnership potential sit mainly on **Facebook and Instagram** (FCBP penya, FCBarca.com, Barca News PL, FC Barcelona Polska), plus TikTok creators.

### Cited Findings
**Football-fan demographics in Poland (general, older data)**
- CBOS 2016: 37% of Polish adults call themselves sports fans, and football is the most popular team sport among them. Men are more than twice as likely as women to call themselves fans (54% vs 21%) — [CBOS 2016](https://cbos.pl/SPISKOM.POL/2016/K_097_16.PDF)
- CBOS 2018: interest in football is declared by 71% of men and 41% of women, and gender is the strongest factor — [CBOS 2018](https://www.cbos.pl/SPISKOM.POL/2018/K_085_18.PDF)
- Homo Homini (date unclear, 15+ population): 45% of Poles are football fans, 63% of men — [Wirtualnemedia](https://www.wirtualnemedia.pl/typowy-polski-kibic-pilkarski-jaki-jest,7172614392243841a)
- Selectivv mobile data from Ekstraklasa stadiums (2018/19): the largest age group was 36–40 (18.52%), and women averaged 21.79% of attendees — [Selectivv](https://selectivv.com/en/what-else-do-we-know-about-ekstraklasa-fan/)
- Fortuna 1 Liga fan profile 2021 (n=7,069): over two thirds men, and almost half aged 25–44 — [1liga.org PDF](https://upload.1liga.org/files/Profil_Kibica_F1L_2021_FINAL1626350317.pdf)

**Platform audiences in Poland, 2026**
- Instagram Poland: about 13.8M users (June 2026 snapshot), 58.8% women, largest age group 25–34 (4.9M). Monthly figures in 2026 ranged from about 12.5M to 13.9M — [NapoleonCat](https://stats.napoleoncat.com/instagram-users-in-poland/)
- TikTok Poland 18+ ad reach (TikTok Ads Manager estimate): about 11.8M, 61.7% female, largest group 25–34 (3.99M), 18–34 = 55% — [Affect Group](https://affectgroup.com/blog/tiktok-ads-audience-in-poland-2026-breakdown/)
- Statista describes Instagram's Polish audience as younger than Facebook's and older than TikTok's — [Statista](https://www.statista.com/statistics/805469/instagram-users-poland)

**Polish Barça communities and creators** (sizes in section 2)
- Fan Club Barça Polska (official penya no. 1782): Facebook about 161k, website fcbp.pl, organises member trips — [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/fcbpolska/); [fcbp.pl](https://fcbp.pl/)
- FCBarca.com: the largest Polish-language Barça site, with Facebook (125.5k), X (@FCBarcacom), YouTube, TikTok (@fcbarcacom), a podcast and a members' forum "La Rambla" — [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/FCBarcacom/); [X](https://x.com/FCBarcacom); [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCz4AQBIaQhgaOmJkLM6CY1w); [La Rambla](https://www.fcbarca.com/la-rambla)
- Blaugrana.pl (linked to the Warsaw penya) — [Blaugrana.pl](https://blaugrana.pl/)
- DumaKatalonii.pl (fan site that lists the fan clubs) — [DumaKatalonii.pl](https://dumakatalonii.pl/fankluby/)
- Barca News PL (Instagram about 63k), linked to the FC Barcelona Polska Facebook page — [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/barcanewspl/)
- BarcaInfo on X (@_BarcaInfo) — [X](https://x.com/_barcainfo)
- TikTok: barca_najlepsza25 has a video with 130.3K likes. "Kibic Barcy" posts humour comparing Barça and Real fans. Follower counts not found — (search snippet summarising TikTok pages, e.g. [TikTok discover](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/jak-sie-zachowuja-pilkarze-a-jak-kibice-realu-i-barcelony))
- natemat.pl describes Polish Barça and Real fan clubs holding meet-ups ("zloty") where they analyse games — [natemat.pl](https://natemat.pl/34109,z-milosci-do-barcy-i-realu-zalozyli-fankluby-w-polsce-na-zlotach-analizuja-gre-ronaldo-i-messiego)

### Inferences
- **Two buyer personas:** (a) the fan himself or herself, mostly male, roughly 16–40, on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube, who buys for himself; (b) the **gift buyer**, often a woman aged 25–44 (partner, mother, sister), who is over-represented on Instagram and TikTok in Poland. The Christmas gift angle should target (b) with interest layers such as "gifts", "jewellery", "football" and "FC Barcelona". The fan angle should target (a) with match-night timing.
- Facebook pages (FCBP 161k, FCBarca.com 125k) suit sponsored posts and giveaways aimed at an older, loyal audience. Instagram and TikTok creators suit native short video. FCBP is an official penya, so check the licensing and club rules before any co-branded activity.

### Gaps
- No age or gender breakdown specific to Polish Barça fans was found. Ask partner accounts for their Instagram/TikTok audience insights before any deal.
- No TikTok follower counts for Polish Barça creators were found. Check the profiles manually, including FCBarca.com's TikTok, barca_najlepsza25 and Kibic Barcy.
- The X (Twitter) user base in Poland for 2026 and Barça-specific YouTube channels were not quantified.
